About Ecliptic

A hands-on field guide

Ecliptic is an interactive guide to our solar system, built for anyone curious about the Sun, the eight planets, and the icy frontier beyond Neptune. Drag the orrery above, or head straight into Explore or Test mode.

Explore covers all ten bodies in depth: real astronomical data and what makes each one distinct, with nothing else mixed in. Test pulls from a bank of solar-system questions plus chemistry, physics, biology and maths questions about the planets, in a length you choose: anywhere from 10 to 200 questions, or any number you type in. Compare puts any set of bodies side by side. Your points, rank, and badges are saved on this device as you go.
